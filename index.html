How to play

Mines are good. Unlike classic minesweeper, you want to click mines. The 16×16 board hides 51 of them. First to capture 26 wins.

Hit a mine, go again. A mine you click gets your flag and you keep playing. Click a safe square and the turn passes to your opponent.

3

Numbers are shared clues. A revealed number tells both players how many mines touch that square. Use them to deduce mines — and remember your opponent sees them too. Revealing numbers helps whoever moves next, so think before you probe.

The bomb. Once per game, when you're not in the lead, you may detonate a 5×5 area. Every mine in the blast becomes yours, safe squares are revealed, then the turn passes. Save it for a rich patch.

★

Strategy basics: when your opponent finds a mine, its neighbors are hot — mines cluster around big numbers. Opening in the middle reveals the most information. A "0" flood-fill gives away a lot of board: sometimes that helps your opponent more than you. (The very first click of a game never opens a flood — starting isn't a punishment.)
